# PyWhiz – Business Requirement Features

## 🎯 Target Audience
- Children aged **11–16 years**
- Students with **limited English language skills**
- Learners who get bored with traditional methods

---

## 🧩 Business Goals
- Provide a **systematic, milestone-based learning path** for coding
- Remove the **English language barrier** in coding education
- Make coding **interactive, fun, and personalized**
- Encourage students to **practice by doing**, not just theory
- Ensure accessibility through a **web-based platform**

---

## 📌 Core Business Features
1. **Milestone-Based Learning**
   - Structured path of 15 milestones  
   - Each milestone introduces new concepts step by step  

2. **Interactive Practice**
   - Code editor with real-time feedback  
   - Exercises: coding tasks, multiple-choice 

3. **Personalized Learning**
   - Adaptive exercises after milestone 7  
   - Personalized tasks based on student performance (logic, syntax, attempts, difficulty level)  

4. **Barrier-Free Learning**
   - Simplified instructions (less dependency on English)  
   - Visual support (videos, animations, and interactive UI)  

5. **Progress Tracking**
   - Dashboard for student progress  
   - Performance-based adjustments in exercise difficulty  

6. **Safe & Inclusive Learning**
   - Kid-friendly design  
   - Respectful code of conduct  

---

## 🚀 Future Business Enhancements
- Multi-language support 
- Gamification (badges, leaderboard, rewards)  
- Teacher dashboard for class monitoring  
- Mobile app version
